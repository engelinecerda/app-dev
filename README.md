# My favorite movie
***The Book of Life** is a 2014 animated fantasy adventure comedy film directed by Jorge R. Gutierrez. The story follows Manolo Sánchez, a young bullfighter torn between fulfilling his family's expectations and following his heart. On the Day of the Dead, he embarks on an adventure that spans three fantastical worlds, facing his greatest fears and discovering the true meaning of courage and love. The film is inspired by Mexican folklore and culture, blending elements of mythology and music into its vibrant storytelling*
